문제 37 | 최초 모델 입력
Objective

Place mirrors and splitters so that sensors S1 and S2 each receive their target color of light.
Each sensor's target is white, red, or blue. The two targets may be the same or different. The target colors are not disclosed.

Grid and source

- The grid is 10 by 10. Row numbers increase downward and column numbers increase to the right. Coordinates are [row, column], with both values from 1 to 10.
- The source emits white light from its given position in its given direction: up, down, left, or right.
- Light first moves to the next cell from the source position. It travels straight through empty cells. A path ends when it leaves the grid.
- When light paths overlap, the beams do not change each other's color or direction.

Filters and sensors

- filters gives the positions and colors of one red filter and one blue filter.
- White light becomes red when it passes through the red filter and blue when it passes through the blue filter.
- Red light passes through the red filter but is blocked by the blue filter, ending that path.
- Blue light passes through the blue filter but is blocked by the red filter, ending that path.
- A filter does not change the direction of transmitted light. White light can reach a sensor without passing through a filter.
- sensors gives the positions of S1 and S2. A light path ends when it reaches a sensor.
- A sensor is correct only if it receives its target color and no other color. No light, a wrong color, or multiple different colors makes that sensor incorrect. Multiple beams of the same color are allowed.

Devices and orientations

- A mirror reflects light. A splitter splits light. Choose each device's type, position, and orientation R or L.
- An R mirror reflects as follows:
  Light moving right (entering from the left) -> up
  Light moving up (entering from below) -> right
  Light moving left (entering from the right) -> down
  Light moving down (entering from above) -> left
- An L mirror reflects as follows:
  Light moving right (entering from the left) -> down
  Light moving down (entering from above) -> right
  Light moving left (entering from the right) -> up
  Light moving up (entering from below) -> left
- A splitter creates two beams: one continues straight and the other reflects as an R or L mirror would, according to the splitter's orientation.

Placement and submission

- Submit the complete device layout in one response. There is no separate limit on the number of mirrors or splitters.
- Every submission starts with no placed devices. Only devices listed in that response are installed; all devices from your previous response are removed.
- You cannot move the source, filters, or sensors, or place a device on any of their current cells. At most one device may occupy a cell.
- The current board is provided before every submission. Always use the source, filter, and sensor positions in the most recently provided board.
- Device coordinates must be integers within the grid.
- Do not use external tools.

Feedback

Use the feedback from each submission to decide your next layout.

Feedback contains sensor_results and result.

sensor_results reports correct or incorrect for S1 and S2 separately. A sensor is correct if it actually receives only its target color; otherwise it is incorrect.

result reports the overall outcome of your submission: correct or incorrect.

If the response format or device layout is invalid and cannot be simulated, both sensors are incorrect and result is incorrect.

Output format

Return a JSON object with exactly one key: placements.
Each item must contain the device type (mirror or splitter), row, col, and direction (R or L).

Example of the submission format with multiple devices:
{"placements":[{"type":"mirror","row":3,"col":5,"direction":"R"},{"type":"splitter","row":6,"col":6,"direction":"L"}]}

Output only the JSON object, without explanations or code fences.
